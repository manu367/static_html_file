FAQ

Questions, answered.

What industries do you work with?

Mostly automotive, EV service networks and D2C/dealer-driven businesses — anywhere operational data needs to become a trustworthy dashboard.

Do you offer white-label dashboard development?

Yes — we build under your brand with your design system, ready to be resold or deployed internally.

How do you ensure data accuracy in dashboards?

Null-safe helper functions, cross-checked SQL vs. aggregation logic, and derived subqueries for accurate closure-time metrics.

Can you migrate our legacy PHP code?

Yes — we regularly move procedural PHP into a clean Controller → Service → Repository architecture without downtime.

Do you provide ongoing support after launch?

Always. Most of our clients have been with us 5–10 years, adding new modules as they grow.

What's your typical project timeline?

A single dashboard module usually ships in 2–4 weeks; full admin panels take 6–10 weeks depending on scope.
